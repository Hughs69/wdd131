Let's Stop Malaria in Africa

Malaria is preventable and treatable. Through awareness, prevention, early testing, and proper treatment, we can protect our families and communities.

Learn How to Prevent Malaria

Why Malaria Matters

Malaria continues to affect millions of people, especially in the African region. Children under five and pregnant women are among those at higher risk of severe illness.

Understand Malaria

Malaria is an illness caused by parasites transmitted through the bites of infected female Anopheles mosquitoes.

Prevent Infection

Using insecticide-treated mosquito nets, avoiding mosquito bites, and supporting mosquito control can reduce risk.

Act Early

Fever and chills can be signs of malaria. Seek testing promptly at a health facility rather than guessing the cause.

Small Actions Can Make a Difference

Protect yourself and your family. Share reliable malaria information with your community.
